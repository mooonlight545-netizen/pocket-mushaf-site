A pocket Qur'an.
Nothing else.

A small e-ink device that displays the authentic Madani mushaf — every word in the King Fahd Complex script, full tashkeel, surah ornaments and all — and remembers exactly where you stopped. No apps, no browser, no notifications, no clock.

Waitlist opening soon Flash a devkit

One device, one purpose

Phones make reading fragile. This device makes it inevitable: open it and you are exactly where you left off, on a paper-like screen that stays readable in sunlight and sips its battery.

The real mushaf

Authentic per-page King Fahd Complex glyphs — the same letterforms as the printed Madani mushaf, not a generic Arabic font. Line breaks, surah bands, and ayah marks where they belong.

Instant resume

Your position is remembered as surah and ayah — never a page number — so it survives every update and every setting change.

Made for e-ink

Quiet partial refreshes while you read, a clean full refresh at just the right rhythm. Designed against real panel behavior from day one.

Truly offline

No radio, no account, no telemetry. Updates happen over a USB cable, on your terms, from this site.

Built for hifz

Hide the lines you are memorizing and reveal them one at a time as you recite. Your memorization state is yours, saved on the device, expressed in ayahs — portable across every future version.

Updates without a connection

The device never touches a network. When there is new firmware, plug in the USB-C cable, open the flash page in Chrome, and click install. That is the whole update system — and it is why there is nothing on this device that can interrupt your reading.
